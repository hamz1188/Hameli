## Order of work (locked)

1. **Trend** — what’s moving globally (not local).
2. **Demo** — build small, public proofs that show you can do that work.
3. **Film** — shoot Working title shorts from those demos (kit + scripts in FILM.md only after demos exist).

Do not film “email me / three questions” content until there is something real to point at.

## Series

## Pillars (3 only)

1. **Portfolio drops** — Live demos you’re willing to show (new builds). Not an archive tour.
2. **Process** — Fix, build, decision moments on the demo work.
3. **Client-path** — How to hire you — only after demos prove the offer.

No fourth pillar until each has **at least 2 posts**.

**Shorts rule:** Do not film or name old portfolio builds. No geo-local trend framing.

## Cadence (after first demos)

- **Minimum:** 1 short (30–90s) per week once demos exist.
- **Miss week fallback:** 30s desk/progress VO on a live demo.
- **Every piece ends with:** CTA → hameli.io / hello@hameli.io (site must list the demos).
