# Working title — how this site is written

Hameli is not a brochure. It is a place to watch how the work is made, then write when you need something built.

## What’s on the home page

1. **Watch** — short scenes (30–90s). Sluglines unlock when a film goes live.
2. **Method** — desk cam, screen, same-day voiceover. One session, three layers.
3. **Write** — `hello@hameli.io`. Say what you’re making.

## Cadence

One short a week is the floor. If a week slips, a 30s desk/progress voiceover still counts. Longer cuts only after shorts are shipping.
