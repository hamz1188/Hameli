# Made in public — how this site works

Hameli is not a sales brochure. It’s a place to **learn how the work is made**, then ask when you need something built.

## What’s on the home page

1. **Watch** — short field notes (30–90s). Rows unlock when a short goes live.
2. **Method** — natural desk cam + screen + same-day voiceover.
3. **Contact** — `hello@hameli.io`. Short notes beat long briefs.

## Cadence

One short a week is the floor. If a week slips, a 30s desk/progress VO still counts. Longer YouTube only after shorts are shipping.
